# Economic Research & Industry Reports

**Investment Research | Economic Analysis | Energy Markets | Data Visualisation**

A collection of professional research publications produced during my time at CBRE, examining commercial real estate investment, energy infrastructure, commodity markets and regional economic development in Alberta, Canada.

These publications demonstrate the application of quantitative research, market analysis and data visualisation to communicate complex economic and industry developments to business audiences.

## Research Publications

### 1. Edmonton Investment Market Report — H2 2021

**Focus:** Commercial Real Estate Investment | Market Trends | Economic Indicators

An analysis of Edmonton's commercial real estate investment market, examining transaction activity, investment volumes, capitalisation rates and economic conditions across industrial, office and retail sectors.

**Key areas of analysis:**
- Historical investment volumes and sector performance
- Property transaction values and capitalisation rates
- Macroeconomic indicators, including GDP, employment and commodity prices
- Infrastructure investment and regional economic outlook

**[View Investment Report (PDF)](CBRE%20Investment%20Report%20H2%202021.pdf)**

### 2. Alberta's Industrial Heartland

**Focus:** Industrial Development | Infrastructure Investment | Regional Economics

Research examining Alberta's Industrial Heartland as a centre for industrial development, energy infrastructure and investment opportunities.

**Key areas of analysis:**
- Regional industrial development and investment activity
- Infrastructure and transportation connectivity
- Petrochemical and energy-sector development
- Emerging opportunities associated with hydrogen and carbon capture

**[View Industrial Heartland Report (PDF)](Alberta%27s%20Industrial%20Heartland.pdf)**

### 3. Alberta Oil & Gas: Transportation at a Glance

**Focus:** Energy Economics | Commodity Markets | Transportation Infrastructure

An industry report examining the economic implications of Alberta's oil transportation infrastructure, including pipeline development, crude-by-rail exports and access to international markets.

**Key areas of analysis:**
- Pipeline capacity and infrastructure investment
- Historical Western Canadian Select (WCS) and West Texas Intermediate (WTI) commodity prices
- Oil export volumes and transportation methods
- Economic implications of the Keystone XL cancellation
- Transportation costs and market accessibility

**[View Oil & Gas Transportation Report (PDF)](Alberta%20Oil%20and%20Gas.pdf)**

### 4. Hydrogen Energy in Alberta

**Focus:** Energy Transition | Hydrogen Economics | Public Policy

Research examining Alberta's emerging hydrogen economy, including its industrial advantages, investment opportunities and the role of carbon capture infrastructure in supporting lower-emissions energy production.

**Key areas of analysis:**
- Hydrogen production and industrial applications
- Carbon capture, utilisation and storage infrastructure
- Government policies and investment incentives
- Regional hydrogen development projects
- Economic opportunities associated with the energy transition

**[View Hydrogen Energy Report (PDF)](Hydrogen-Energy-In-Alberta.pdf)**
